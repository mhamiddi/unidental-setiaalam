## Copywriting Direction
- **Tone:** Mesra, professional, comforting. Malay-English rojak minimal — mostly BM.
- **Headline:** "Senyuman Lebih Yakin Bermula Di Sini." — aspirational, comfort-focused
- **Subhead:** Addresses the fear directly — "pendekatan lembut & mesra"
- **CTA pattern:** Soft (Free Consultation) → Medium (Treatment-specific WhatsApp) → Hard (Call Now)
- **No hard sell language.** Position: "kami di sini untuk bantu — bukan untuk jual"

## CTA Strategy (Graduated Commitment)

| Level | Location | Text | Intent |
|-------|----------|------|--------|
| Soft | Hero primary | "WhatsApp Konsultasi Percuma" | Low risk, no commitment |
| Medium | Each treatment card | "Tanya Harga [Treatment]" | Contextual, warm |
| Medium | Booking section | "Tempah Konsultasi Percuma" | Mid-funnel |
| Hard | Final CTA | "WhatsApp Sekarang" | Close |
| Always | Floating mobile bar | "WhatsApp / Call" | Persistent |
| Backup | Hero secondary | "Call Terus" | Alternative contact |

## Pitfalls

- Don't include pricing if client wants to hide it
- Medical disclaimer is mandatory in footer
- WhatsApp pre-fill MUST include treatment name — never generic "nak tanya pasal gigi"
- "Konsultasi percuma" must actually be free — if not, change to "Konsultasi"
